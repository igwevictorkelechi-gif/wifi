Measuring with a phone

How do I see dBm on my phone?

iPhone

  1. Install Apple's free AirPort Utility app from the App Store.
  2. Open iPhone Settings › Apps › AirPort Utility and turn on Wi-Fi Scanner.
  3. Open AirPort Utility, tap Wi-Fi Scan (top right), then Scan.
  4. Read the RSSI next to your network, e.g. −58 dBm.

Android

  1. Install a free analyzer such as WiFiman or WiFi Analyzer (open-source).
  2. Open it while connected to your WiFi; it shows your signal in dBm.

Then, for each spot

  1. Stand still, phone at chest height, and wait about 5 seconds for the number to settle.
  2. Here, pick the Reading tool, click where you stood, and type the number.
  3. Take 2 or 3 readings per room, including the far corners.

Closer to zero is stronger: −45 is excellent, −67 is fine for video, −80 is barely usable.
